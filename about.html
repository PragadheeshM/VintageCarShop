Who We Are

Preserving Automotive Heritage

Founded in 1987; Vintage Car Restoration Shop has grown from a small garage operation into one of the most respected classic car restoration facilities in the country. Our founder, Richard Hayes, started with a single-bay workshop and a burning passion for vintage automobiles.

Today, our 20,000 sq ft facility houses state-of-the-art equipment alongside traditional hand tools, because some things should never change. Every car that enters our shop receives the same meticulous attention to detail that has earned us over 45 awards.
