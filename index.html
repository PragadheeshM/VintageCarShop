What We Do

Our Restoration Services

From complete frame-off restorations to custom paint and upholstery, we offer a full spectrum of services for your classic automobile.

Full Restoration

Complete frame-off restoration bringing your classic back to showroom condition with exacting attention to every detail.

Custom Paint

Period-correct color matching and custom finishes using the finest automotive paints and clear coats available.

Engine Rebuild

Complete engine overhaul and performance tuning to restore original power while maintaining authenticity.

Interior Upholstery

Hand-stitched leather and period-correct fabric restoration to bring your interior back to its original luxury.

Electrical Systems

Complete rewiring with modern reliability while maintaining the authentic look of original wiring harnesses.

Inspection & Appraisal

Professional pre-purchase inspections and certified appraisals for insurance and auction valuations.
