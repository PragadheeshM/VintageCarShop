Common Questions

What determines the final cost?
The final cost depends on the vehicle's current condition, the scope of work, parts availability, and the level of finish desired. We provide a detailed estimate after the initial assessment.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. Typically, a deposit is required at the start, with milestone-based payments throughout the restoration process.
Can I upgrade mid-restoration?
Absolutely. Many clients start with a Standard package and upgrade as they see the progress. We're flexible and transparent about any cost adjustments.
