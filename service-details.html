Full Frame-Off Restoration

Home/Services/Full Restoration

The Ultimate Restoration Experience

Our full frame-off restoration is the pinnacle of classic car restoration. Every component is carefully removed, cataloged, and either restored or replaced with period-correct parts. The body is separated from the frame, both are media-blasted, and the entire vehicle is rebuilt from the ground up.

This process typically takes 12-18 months depending on the vehicle's condition and the level of customization desired. Our team documents every step with detailed photography so you can follow along with your car's journey back to glory.

What's Included

Complete disassembly & cataloging
Media blasting & rust treatment
Body & panel straightening
Period-correct paint application
Engine rebuild & dyno testing
Transmission overhaul
Complete interior restoration
Full photo documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full restoration take?
A complete frame-off restoration typically takes 12-18 months, depending on the vehicle's condition, parts availability, and level of customization. We provide regular updates and detailed timelines throughout the process.
Do you source original parts?
Yes, we have an extensive network of suppliers for NOS (New Old Stock) and reproduction parts. We always prioritize original parts and will source them globally to ensure authenticity.
Can I visit during the restoration?
Absolutely! We encourage owners to visit and see their vehicle's progress. We also provide a secure online portal with weekly photo updates so you can follow along remotely.
Do you offer warranty on restorations?
Yes, all full restorations come with a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering all mechanical work and a 5-year warranty on paint and bodywork.
